# Glossary

A simple dictionary for HoopVision terms.

## Basketball Terms

**Game**  
One basketball game between two teams.

**Team**  
A group of players playing in the game.

**Player**  
A person on a team.

**Lineup**  
The five players from one team who are on the court at the same time.

**Starter**  
A player who begins the game on the court.

**Substitution**  
When one player leaves the court and another player enters.

## Analytics Terms

**Plus/Minus**  
How many points a player's team gained or lost while they were on the court.

**Possession**  
One chance for a team to try to score.

**Offensive Rating**  
How many points a team scores per 100 possessions.

**Defensive Rating**  
How many points a team allows per 100 possessions.

**Net Rating**  
Offensive rating minus defensive rating.

**On/Off**  
Comparing how the team performs when a player is on the court versus off the court.

Important: on/off does not prove the player caused the difference. It only shows an association.
